How to read this

Every figure is an estimate. Freight is computed from simple rate functions (distance, weight, a minimum charge, weight breaks and a fuel surcharge) that stand in for carrier contracts; they are illustrative, not real tariffs. Road miles are straight-line miles times 1.18. Transit days come from distance and mode. The plan assumes demand like the weeks supplied, and does not count the cost of changing a customer's DC, inventory, or carrier capacity. Check the largest changes against real quotes before acting on them.
